CLOUDSCAPE: SLOW FLIGHT

Lively Wallpaper: drag the intact ZIP into its window.
For a browser preview, extract the ZIP first and open index.html in Edge.
Keep the settings hidden for a clean wallpaper; H reveals or hides them in the browser preview.
Flight, clouds, terrain and lighting are generated in real time, not played from a recording.
Medium quality and up to 30 FPS by default. WebGL 2 + EXT_color_buffer_float required.
If desired, use Lively's performance settings to pause the wallpaper when windows cover it.

Original Cloudscape: https://github.com/Fishypants/cloudscape
MIT license; see LICENSE.txt. This package adds a looping camera trajectory,
a clean default layout, and wallpaper-friendly rendering settings.
